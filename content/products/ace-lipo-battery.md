<!-- locale:en -->
Gens ACE LiPo batteries for drone soccer and FPV racing. The 200 and 220 brushless soccer drones VLI supplies (TOPS Shield 205/220) run on 4S packs: choose the OVONIC 4S or TATTU 4S battery for them. The 3S 11.1V 1100mAh pack is for 3S builds and FPV racing drones; the 4S and 6S 3000mAh 120C packs suit 400 brushless soccer drones.

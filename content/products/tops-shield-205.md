<!-- locale:en -->
Engineered for high-intensity drone sports, the TOPS Shield 205 combines a lightweight 205mm spherical exoskeleton with a competition-tuned powertrain. Designed to bounce off arena walls and opponents without dropping from the sky, it is the ultimate platform for tight-quarters maneuverability.

# VLI website pricing

Every price shown on velocity-lab.com is set in this file.

**How to edit:** click the pencil icon, update the applicable tier-price column, then **Commit changes** to `main`. The website updates in about a minute.

- Type prices exactly as they should appear, e.g. `HK$4,329`. Product cards show the Tier 1 starting price; product detail dialogs expose Tier 1 and Tier 2 when both are supplied.
- Do not change the **ID** column. The other columns are labels to help you find the right row.
- **Tier 1 min. qty** is the minimum order quantity when a customer chooses Tier 1 – PARTS only (e.g. `2`). Leave it blank for no minimum. Tier 2 has no minimum, so a customer who wants a single drone chooses Tier 2.
- Every product version needs a row here. If a row is missing or an ID is unknown, the update fails and the website keeps the previous prices. Leave Tier 2 and VLI-CARE blank when the item is sold only as Tier 1.

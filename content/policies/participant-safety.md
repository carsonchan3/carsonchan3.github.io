## Waiver Summary

This safety policy and liability waiver applies to all individuals, students, teachers, coaches, tournament pilots, and spectators participating in workshops, building sessions, flight training courses, Smart Referee trials, and drone soccer competitions organized or delivered by Velocity Lab Innovation ("VLI").

Drone sports involve specialized robotics, high-speed electric motors spinning at up to 40,000 RPM, sharp propellers, high-discharge lithium batteries, and forceful projectile collisions inside designated flight enclosures. Safety requires strict adherence to operating discipline, personal protective equipment (PPE), and venue protocols.

## Mandatory Safety Zone Regulations

All VLI training sessions, school STEM courses, and competition venues must enforce the following safety zones:

| Zone / Operational Area | Mandatory Safety Rule | Enforcement & Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flight Cage / Playing Arena | 100% enclosed protective netting (mesh ≤50mm) covering all sides and ceiling. | Strictly zero human entry while any aircraft is armed or powered on. Enclosure doors must be latched before arming. |
| Flight Cage / Playing Arena / Battery Charging | A fire extinguisher or water bucket shall be accessible during usage of drones. | LiPo batteries, if handled incorrectly, can be a fire hazard, and precautions shall be taken. |
| Technical Bench & Staging | Flat, clean workshop surface with dedicated tool staging. | Aircraft must be disarmed, and batteries unplugged during all mechanical assembly, soldering, or firmware flashing. |
| Battery Charging Station | Dedicated, well-ventilated area isolated from main student traffic. | Multi-channel chargers (e.g., TATTU TA300) must operate on fire-retardant mats. Batteries must be housed in fireproof LiPo bags. |

## Participant Safety Conduct Code

All participants agree to abide by the following mandatory safety rules:

- **Arming Discipline:** Drones must NEVER be armed (motors spun up) outside the protective flight netting or designated test enclosure. Propellers must be completely removed when testing flight controller configurations or motor direction on the workshop bench.
- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Safety glasses or goggles must be worn whenever handling powered aircraft or soldering equipment. Loose hair, ties, and lanyard badges must be secured.
- **Immediate Cease-Flight ("Disarm") Command:** If any spectator, student, or animal breaches the perimeter, or if protective netting is damaged, the instructor or referee will call "DISARM" or "CUT THROTTLE". All pilots must immediately disarm aircraft.
- **Physical Cage Recovery:** After a match or flight session, pilots must visually confirm that all aircraft are disarmed before opening the netting door. Powered aircraft must be handled strictly by the outer cage frame, avoiding internal propellers.

## Assumption of Inherent Risk

Participants, parents, and school representatives acknowledge that participating in drone sports, FPV piloting, and technical workshops entails inherent risks, including but not limited to:

- Physical impact from aircraft escaping containment due to structural failure or net damage.
- Minor cuts, abrasions, or pinch injuries from rotating propellers.
- Thermal burns from soldering irons, hot electronic components, or short-circuited batteries.
- Smoke inhalation or chemical exposure in the event of a damaged LiPo battery cell.

By registering for or participating in VLI programmes, participants freely and voluntarily assume all such inherent operational risks.

For courses delivered at primary, secondary, or tertiary educational institutions:

- School teachers and designated institutional supervisors retain primary supervisory responsibility and pastoral duty of care for participating students at all times.
- VLI instructors provide technical instruction, equipment coordination, and flight safety enforcement, but do not act in place of authorized school chaperones.
- VLI instructors reserve the right to immediately suspend any participant whose conduct threatens personal safety or violates workshop protocols.

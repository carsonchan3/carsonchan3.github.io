## Policy Summary

Velocity Lab Innovation ("VLI", "we", "us", or "our") respects the personal data privacy of all clients, website visitors, workshop participants, and institutional contacts. This policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, retention, transfer, and security of personal data in compliance with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486, Laws of Hong Kong) ("PDPO").

This document includes our statutory Personal Information Collection Statement ("PICS"), provided in accordance with Data Protection Principle 1(3) of the PDPO.

## Personal Information Collection Statement (PICS)

When you interact with VLI—including submitting an inquiry on [velocity-lab.com/product](/product), utilizing the online quote cart, booking a workshop on [velocity-lab.com/services](/services), or contacting us directly—we may collect personal data from you.

| Data Category | Examples Collected | Primary Purpose of Collection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact Information | Name, email address, telephone number | Communicating regarding inquiries, quotes, and delivery updates. |
| Institutional Affiliation | School name, club name, company, role | Verifying institutional eligibility, EDB grant tenders, and B2B terms. |
| Operational / Project Scope | Class size, age group, venue details, dates | Scoping curriculum delivery, venue safety, and drone fleet sizing. |
| Transaction & Billing Data | Billing address, payment receipts, PO numbers | Processing commercial invoices, warranty registrations, and accounting. |

## Purpose of Data Collection

Personal data collected by VLI will be used strictly for the following purposes:

- Assessing equipment and service inquiries and preparing tailored commercial quotations.
- Processing, fulfilling, and delivering orders for drone hardware, power systems, and replacement parts.
- Administering warranty registrations, RMA repair requests, and VLI-CARE annual service contracts.
- Planning and delivering drone education courses, teacher training workshops, and competition operations.
- Complying with statutory requirements under Hong Kong aviation or financial reporting laws.

## Voluntary Provision and Impact of Non-Provision

The provision of personal data to VLI is voluntary. However, where requested data is marked as mandatory or necessary for transaction processing (e.g., recipient contact details for equipment courier delivery or billing verification), failure to provide such data may prevent VLI from issuing a formal quotation, fulfilling an order, or confirming workshop bookings.

## Disclosure and Transfer of Personal Data

Personal data held by VLI is kept strictly confidential. VLI does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may disclose or transfer personal data solely to the following classes of third parties on a strict need-to-know basis:

- Logistical service providers and courier companies (e.g., for shipping drone hardware and battery packs within Hong Kong).
- Financial institutions, payment processors, and banking partners (e.g., HSBC Commercial Banking) for payment settlement and audit compliance.
- Technical service contractors engaged to support website hosting, database operations, or workshop delivery, bound by confidentiality obligations.
- Regulatory bodies, law enforcement, or government agencies where mandated by law or court order.

## Direct Marketing Policy

VLI complies with Part 6A of the PDPO regarding direct marketing. We will not use your personal data to send promotional newsletters, product launch announcements, or tournament invitations without your explicit prior consent.

If you have provided consent, you maintain the right to withdraw consent and opt out of direct marketing communications at any time, free of charge, by emailing [info@velocity-lab.com](mailto:info@velocity-lab.com) with the subject "Unsubscribe".

## Data Retention and Security Safeguards

VLI retains personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or to satisfy legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory requirements. Commercial and invoice records are typically retained for seven (7) years in accordance with Hong Kong tax laws.

We implement robust physical, technical, and managerial safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorized access, accidental loss, processing, alteration, or disclosure. All digital transmissions via VLI portals use encrypted protocols.

## Cookies and Web Analytics

VLI digital portals use functional and performance cookies to maintain session states, manage the quote cart, and collect anonymized website usage analytics. You may configure your browser settings to refuse or delete cookies; however, certain portal functions (such as the equipment quote cart) may experience limited functionality.

## Data Subject Rights: Access and Correction

Under the PDPO, you have the right to request access to and correction of your personal data held by VLI. To exercise your rights, please submit a written request to:

> **Data Protection Liaison**, Velocity Lab Innovation
> Email: [info@velocity-lab.com](mailto:info@velocity-lab.com) · Phone: +852 6650 7520
> Subject: "Data Access Request / Data Correction Request (PDPO)"

In accordance with the PDPO, VLI may charge a reasonable administrative fee for processing data access requests.

## General Terms

VLI reserves the right to review and update this Privacy Policy and PICS periodically to reflect evolving legal requirements and operational practices. The updated version will be published on our website with the revised effective date. Continued engagement with VLI following published updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

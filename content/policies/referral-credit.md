## 1. Policy Purpose

Velocity Lab Innovation ("VLI", "we", "us", or "our") values long-term institutional relationships that expand safe, practical, and sustainable drone-sports and technical education programmes. This B2B Referral and Service Credit Policy (the "Policy") explains how eligible education organisations may receive and use referral service credits in connection with qualifying VLI purchases.

The Policy is designed to support programme adoption, teacher development, technical readiness, maintenance, and student activity. It is strictly an institutional credit programme and does not constitute a cash rebate, sales commission, or personal incentive programme.

## 2. Institutional Eligibility

This Policy is available exclusively to incorporated or formally recognised education organisations, including primary schools, secondary schools, universities, NGOs, youth organisations, community organisations, or registered education centres (each an "Institution"). An Institution may participate only through an authorised representative acting strictly within that Institution's purchasing and ethics governance frameworks.

An Institution may submit a referral where it introduces VLI to another prospective education organisation (the "Referred Institution") that has not, prior to the referral submission date:

- Purchased products or services from VLI;
- Received a formal VLI quotation for the relevant programme within the preceding six (6) months;
- Been actively engaged by VLI through an existing sales opportunity, tender, request for quotation, or formal procurement process; or
- Been previously referred to VLI by another Institution or authorized partner.

VLI will determine, acting reasonably and using its internal customer records, whether a Referred Institution is eligible. VLI's eligibility determination is final for the purposes of this Policy.

## 3. Referral Submission Workflow

The referring Institution must submit the referral before, or at the time, VLI first receives the Referred Institution's substantive inquiry.

Referrals must be submitted by email to [info@velocity-lab.com](mailto:info@velocity-lab.com) with the subject line "Institutional Referral - [Referred Institution Name]" and must include:

- The Referred Institution's legal and operational name.
- The authorized contact person's official business email and telephone number.
- A concise description of the prospective programme, STEM course, or equipment area of interest.

The referring Institution confirms that it possesses legitimate authority to make the introduction and has obtained the contact person's prior consent to share their details with VLI. Neither the referring Institution nor its representative should disclose unnecessary personal data or confidential procurement materials.

## 4. Qualifying Referrals and Credit Amounts

A referral becomes a "Qualifying Referral" only when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

- VLI formally accepts the referral as eligible under the criteria set out in Section 2;
- The Referred Institution executes its first paid commercial contract or purchase order with VLI of at least HK$5,000 in net order value, excluding shipping, travel, venue fees, taxes (if any), and reimbursable third-party expenses;
- The Referred Institution has settled all invoice amounts due under that initial qualifying order in full; and
- The equipment, service, or training programme has been delivered and completed without cancellation, refund, chargeback, material payment default, or unresolved dispute.

Once a Qualifying Referral is confirmed, VLI will issue service credits in the names of the respective Institutions for application toward their next purchase:

| Recipient | Credit Amount | Issue Timing | Permitted Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Referring Institution | HK$1,500 | Within 30 days after Qualifying Referral confirmation | Eligible services, technical support, teacher training, PID optimisation, repair assessment, labour, and eligible spare parts. |
| Referred Institution | HK$1,000 | Within 30 days after Qualifying Referral confirmation | Eligible launch sessions, teacher development, technical support, fleet maintenance, and spare-parts packages. |

VLI may, at its discretion, confirm a modified credit amount in a written promotional notice or formal commercial quotation. Any such written notice will prevail solely for the specific campaign or quotation to which it expressly relates.

## 5. Permitted Use of Service Credits

Service credits may be redeemed exclusively against VLI products and services approved at the time of quotation. Unless otherwise confirmed in writing, credits may be applied toward:

| Eligible Category | Approved Scope and Service Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Professional Development & Training | Remote teacher launch sessions, on-site teacher workshops, building and coaching sessions, advanced adult/teacher training courses, and student launch events. |
| Technical Services | PID flight-control optimisation, maintenance retunes, remote technical troubleshooting, workshop repair diagnostics, approved technician repair labour, and programme technical reviews. |
| Programme Support & Maintenance | 90-day launch support, annual technical support agreements, priority programme assistance, scheduled fleet health checks, and annual maintenance allowances. |
| Spare Parts & Consumables | Pre-approved spare-parts starter packs, replacement propellers, protective cage panels, carbon plates, fasteners, and other high-wear programme consumables. |
| Optional Event Services | Tournament coordination, scrimmage refereeing support, event launch operations, and approved school-event photo or cinematography services. |

A service credit may be applied to up to 20% of the total value of an eligible spare-parts or expansion-hardware order, provided that VLI confirms the order remains commercially viable and the credit is itemized on the written quotation. This hardware flexibility does not apply to initial core drone kits, transmitters, flight batteries, smart chargers, competition arenas, discounted bundle packages, public tender pricing, or products where the credit would reduce the hardware margin below VLI's approved threshold.

## 6. Exclusions and Restrictions

Service credits are non-cash instruments and cannot be exchanged, refunded, transferred, sold, assigned, pledged, or redeemed for cash. Credits may not be used for or in conjunction with:

- The initial drone kit, controller, battery, charger, arena enclosure, or core hardware included in the Referred Institution's first qualifying order;
- Quotations already subject to tender pricing, formal public procurement procedures, special distributor discounts, clearance sales, or concurrent promotional campaigns, unless expressly approved in writing;
- Freight, delivery, courier charges, instructor travel expenses, venue rental, insurance, statutory permits, third-party contractor fees, or unapproved replacement parts;
- Credits previously expired, cancelled, or revoked under this Policy; or
- Equipment or services not listed in VLI's current service catalogue or active quotation price book.

VLI will not issue a service credit where it reasonably determines that the referral represents a self-referral, duplicate entry, artificial arrangement, improper inducement, or breach of either Institution's purchasing or anti-corruption regulations.

## 7. Validity, Redemption, and Limits

Each service credit is valid for twelve (12) months from its formal issue date and must be redeemed against a qualifying VLI quotation or invoice prior to expiration. Any unused credit balance expires automatically upon the 12-month anniversary and will not be extended, refunded, or carried forward without prior written approval.

An Institution may receive a maximum cumulative total of HK$6,000 in referral service credits in any rolling 12-month period under this Policy. VLI may apply an approved credit in full or in part against an eligible invoice. Any remaining balance remains valid until fully drawn down or expired.

A service credit cannot reduce the net invoice total of any item below zero, nor may it be applied as an order deposit or used to offset overdue account balances. The Institution remains responsible for all invoice charges not covered by the credit in accordance with standard VLI payment terms.

## 8. Institutional Compliance and Ethical Conduct

All referral service credits are issued strictly in the legal name of the Institution and never to an individual teacher, employee, principal, school board member, procurement officer, volunteer, or personal representative. VLI strictly prohibits the offering or provision of personal cash, vouchers, gifts, kickbacks, or personal advantages in connection with institutional referrals.

Before submitting a referral or redeeming a credit, the Institution must ensure that participation complies fully with its own procurement guidelines, conflict-of-interest disclosures, gift policies, and statutory regulations under the Prevention of Bribery Ordinance (Cap. 201). The Institution is solely responsible for securing internal approvals and maintaining required institutional accounting records.

VLI reserves the right to request written certification confirming compliance. VLI may withhold, suspend, or cancel credits where issuance could create an actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest or compromise public procurement integrity.

## 9. Programme Relationship and Brand Usage Terms

Separately from referral credits, VLI may offer relationship-building benefits such as collaborative case studies, pilot demonstrations, peer references, technical review sessions, or co-branded launch events. These discretionary benefits must be agreed upon in writing and do not obligate the Institution to endorse VLI, provide commercial testimonials, or purchase additional services.

By participating in this Policy or redeeming a service credit, the Institution grants VLI a non-exclusive, royalty-free, revocable license to display the Institution's official name, logo, and general partnership affiliation on VLI's digital portal, pitch decks, and educational reports solely to identify the Institution as a participating education partner or client.

VLI agrees to reproduce the Institution's logo faithfully in accordance with any provided brand guidelines. VLI will not use the Institution's marks in any manner implying formal endorsement, legal sponsorship, or liability beyond the scope of this Policy.

Any public release of photographs, video recordings, student imagery, or project footage captured on the Institution's premises requires prior written authorization and appropriate parental/guardian consent.

The Institution may revoke brand usage permission at any time by emailing [info@velocity-lab.com](mailto:info@velocity-lab.com). VLI will remove the logo from digital portals within fourteen (14) business days and cease its incorporation into future printed collateral.

## 10. Cancellation, Reversal, and Suspension

VLI reserves the right to cancel, reverse, suspend, or refuse redemption of any service credit where:

- The associated qualifying order is cancelled, refunded, charged back, substantially reduced, or unpaid;
- The referral was submitted after VLI had already initiated substantive contact with the Referred Institution;
- Information supplied in connection with the referral is found to be materially inaccurate, misleading, or fraudulent;
- VLI reasonably suspects procurement abuse, collusion, improper advantage, or policy violations; or
- The Institution breaches a material term of its commercial agreement, quotation, or invoice terms with VLI.

Where a credit has already been credited against an invoice and is subsequently reversed, VLI will issue an adjusted invoice for the corresponding sum, accompanied by a written explanation.

## 11. No Agency, Partnership, or Exclusivity

Participation in this Policy does not establish an agency, partnership, joint venture, employer-employee relationship, franchise, or commercial distributorship between VLI and any participating Institution. Neither party possesses authority to bind the other or enter into commitments on the other's behalf. Participating Institutions retain complete independence regarding supplier selection.

## 12. Policy Amendments

VLI may amend, suspend, or withdraw this Policy at any time through written notification, website announcement, or publication of an updated version. Changes will not retroactively affect credits legitimately issued prior to the revision date, except where necessary to address legal, regulatory, anti-corruption, or security compliance.

## 13. Governing Law and Inquiries

This Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. Any dispute arising under or in connection with this Policy shall be addressed through good-faith consultation between authorized representatives, failing which the courts of Hong Kong SAR shall have non-exclusive jurisdiction.

All inquiries regarding referral submissions, eligibility verification, or credit balances should be directed to Velocity Lab Innovation at [info@velocity-lab.com](mailto:info@velocity-lab.com) or +852 6650 7520.

## 14. Institutional Acceptance

An Institution confirms its acceptance of this Policy by submitting a formal referral, accepting an issued service credit, requesting credit redemption on an invoice, or otherwise participating in the programme. Participation constitutes confirmation that the Institution's authorized representative has read, understood, and agreed to these terms.

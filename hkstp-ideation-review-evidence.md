# HKSTP Ideation Review Evidence

## Official programme context

The official HKSTP programme pages state that Ideation is a one-year programme for HKID holders aged 18 or above with a ground-breaking idea proven through R&D and business planning. The programme offers funding, mentorship, co-working space, and startup support. HKSTP states that up to HKD 100,000 is disbursed across three tranches, subject to satisfactory assessment of three milestones. The current published intake process includes application submission, a panel assessment for shortlisted applicants, and an online due-diligence meeting.

## Review implications

The grantor review should therefore test whether the proposal demonstrates a genuine technology and R&D plan rather than a project-integration or service offer; a credible customer problem and business plan; a milestone-based plan with measurable technical and commercial outputs; and founders who can progress through diligence and delivery.
